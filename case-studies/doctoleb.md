**An offline-first product for the daily work of a clinic.**

Personal project · In development

## The problem

Appointments, patient records, and visit administration are connected parts of a clinic's day. A product for that environment must fit how doctors and reception teams work and account for unreliable connectivity.

## The product direction

Doctoleb is designed to bring those workflows into one application, with local operation as a core architectural choice. The interface is intended for English, Arabic, and French, including right-to-left Arabic layouts.

The design distinguishes the responsibilities of receptionists, doctors, and administrators rather than treating every staff member as the same user.

## My focus

- Translate clinic needs into concrete product requirements and implementation tasks.
- Design local-first workflows with synchronization as an explicit concern.
- Keep staff responsibilities and data visibility part of the product architecture.
- Build clear interfaces for day-to-day operations across languages.

## Engineering approach

The project uses a React and TypeScript progressive web application, local IndexedDB storage, shared validation, and a Node-based synchronization architecture. Unit, integration, and end-to-end checks are part of the development plan.

This page presents the product's scope and design approach. It does not claim a completed production rollout, verified clinical outcomes, or a particular compliance certification. No patient information or private application source is published here.
